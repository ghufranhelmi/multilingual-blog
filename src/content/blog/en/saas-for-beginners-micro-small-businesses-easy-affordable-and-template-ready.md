## Introduction

Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) has become the most practical entry point for beginners and micro-to-small businesses that want to sell online without hiring developers or managing servers. Instead of buying software licenses and maintaining infrastructure, users subscribe to a cloud platform, log in through a browser, and start building a website, online store, or business workflow immediately.

For a first-time entrepreneur, the ideal SaaS platform should reduce three barriers: technical complexity, upfront cost, and time to launch. Platforms such as **Shopify (Basic plan)**, **Wix**, and **SIRCLO Store** address these needs through no-code editors, subscription pricing, and ready-made templates.

## What SaaS Means for Small Businesses

SaaS is software delivered over the internet on a subscription basis. The provider hosts the application, handles updates, security patches, backups, and server infrastructure, while the business focuses on products, customers, and sales.

For micro and small businesses, this model is attractive because it converts a large capital expense into a predictable monthly operating cost. A business can start with a low-tier plan, test demand, and upgrade only when sales volume, staff, or operational complexity increases.

### Key benefits

* **No-code operation:** Business owners can create pages, upload products, and process orders without programming.
* **Low upfront cost:** Most platforms offer free trials, free tiers, or entry-level monthly plans.
* **Fast launch:** Templates, drag-and-drop editors, and guided setup reduce launch time from weeks to days.
* **Built-in maintenance:** Hosting, security updates, and platform performance are managed by the vendor.
* **Scalability:** Businesses can add payment methods, shipping options, marketing tools, and sales channels as they grow.

## Shopify Basic

### Pricing and plans

Shopify Basic is designed for solo entrepreneurs and businesses launching their first online store. Shopify lists the Basic plan at **US$29 per month when billed annually** and **US$39 per month when billed monthly**, with card processing starting from **2.9% plus US$0.30** per online transaction through Shopify Payments in the United States. \[web:3]

New merchants can begin with a **3-day free trial** without a credit card. After the trial, Shopify advertises a promotional rate of **US$1 per month for the first three months** in eligible markets, followed by standard pricing. Shopify states that there are no setup fees on its plans. \[web:3]

For an Indonesian business, the final price, available payment providers, and card-processing fees should be checked on Shopify’s local pricing page because rates and currencies differ by market. \[web:2]

### Ease of use

Shopify is more structured than a general website builder because it is built specifically for commerce. Users manage products, inventory, orders, discounts, customer data, and shipping from one dashboard.

The platform is no-code for common tasks: adding products, changing themes, editing navigation, creating discount codes, and connecting domains can be done through menus and forms. More advanced customization may require Liquid theme editing or a developer, but most beginners can launch with a pre-built theme.

### Customer support

Shopify provides a Help Center, community forums, guides, and support channels that vary by plan and region. Its large user base also means abundant third-party tutorials, agencies, and developer resources.

The main limitation for some Indonesian beginners is that support and much of the learning material are primarily in English. Businesses that need Indonesian-language onboarding may prefer a local platform or a Shopify partner.

### Integrations

Shopify’s strongest advantage is its app ecosystem. Merchants can connect payment gateways, shipping providers, accounting tools, email marketing, review apps, POS systems, and marketplace channels.

Shopify also supports selling across online stores, in-person sales through POS, and AI chat channels, making it suitable for businesses that expect to expand beyond a single website. \[web:3]

**Best for:** Businesses that expect eCommerce to become their main sales channel and want a scalable, globally recognized platform.

## Wix

### Pricing and plans

Wix is one of the most accessible options for absolute beginners because it offers a **free plan** with Wix branding and a Wix subdomain. Paid plans begin at **US$17 per month** on annual billing for the Light plan. \[web:8]

For online selling, the relevant entry point is usually the **Core plan**, priced at **US$29 per month annually**. It includes payment acceptance, basic eCommerce, scheduling, 50 GB of storage, a free domain for one year, and up to five site collaborators. \[web:7]

| Wix plan       | Annual-billing price | Main use                                               |
| -------------- | -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Free           | US$0                 | Learning, drafts, and basic websites with Wix branding |
| Light          | US$17/month          | Simple business websites and custom domains            |
| Core           | US$29/month          | Basic eCommerce and payment acceptance                 |
| Business       | US$39/month          | Standard eCommerce and marketing needs                 |
| Business Elite | US$159/month         | Advanced eCommerce and larger teams                    |

Prices vary by country, billing period, and taxes. Monthly billing is more expensive than annual billing; for example, Wix lists Core at US$36 per month when billed monthly. \[web:10]

### Ease of use

Wix is arguably the easiest platform for a complete beginner. Its drag-and-drop editor allows users to place text, images, buttons, forms, and sections freely on a page.

Wix also provides an AI website builder that can generate a starting site based on answers to a few business questions. For a micro business without a designer, this can dramatically reduce setup time.

The trade-off is flexibility: highly customized layouts and complex eCommerce operations may become harder to manage as the business grows. Wix is excellent for simplicity, but Shopify is usually stronger for inventory-heavy retail operations.

### Customer support

Wix offers a support center, tutorials, community resources, and customer support options. Its interface and educational materials are designed for non-technical users, which lowers the learning curve.

However, support quality and language availability can differ by region. Indonesian users should verify whether local-language assistance is available before committing to an annual plan.

### Integrations

Wix includes native tools for forms, booking, email marketing, SEO basics, analytics, and payments. Its App Market adds third-party tools for reviews, live chat, accounting, shipping, and marketing.

For simple stores, Wix’s built-in tools are usually sufficient. For complex operations—such as multichannel marketplace synchronization or advanced inventory workflows—businesses may need additional apps or a more commerce-focused platform.

**Best for:** Beginners, home-based businesses, service providers, creators, and small retailers that prioritize visual design and simple setup.

## SIRCLO Store

### Pricing and plans

SIRCLO Store is an Indonesian SaaS eCommerce platform aimed at local small and medium businesses. SIRCLO describes it as a template-based online-store platform that lets businesses create an online store, integrate marketplaces, payments, and delivery services in one dashboard. \[web:22]

SIRCLO advertises subscription options **starting from Rp 0**, making it particularly relevant for UMKM that want to test digital sales with minimal upfront spending. \[web:23] However, paid package pricing is not always fully transparent on public pages and may depend on selected features, product volume, marketplace integrations, and support level. Independent comparisons estimate entry-level paid packages around Rp 275,000 to Rp 375,000 per month, but these figures should be treated as indicative rather than official quotes. \[web:19]

### Ease of use

SIRCLO Store is designed to make launching an online store “as easy as creating a blog,” according to SIRCLO’s own messaging. \[web:28] Its template-first approach means a seller can select a design, add products, configure payment and delivery, and begin selling without coding.

The platform is especially practical for Indonesian sellers because its dashboard is built around local selling behavior: marketplace management, local payment methods, delivery services, and WhatsApp-based chat commerce. \[web:18]

### Customer support

SIRCLO’s local presence is a meaningful advantage for Indonesian UMKM. The company positions SIRCLO Store specifically for local businesses and provides solutions connected to Indonesian marketplaces, payments, logistics, and chat commerce. \[web:22]

For beginners who prefer Bahasa Indonesia support, local case studies, and assistance with domestic marketplace operations, SIRCLO may be easier to adopt than global platforms. Businesses with complex needs should ask SIRCLO directly about onboarding, response times, and whether dedicated support is included in their chosen package.

### Integrations

SIRCLO Store’s major differentiator is marketplace integration. SIRCLO states that its platform integrates with nine marketplaces: Tokopedia, Shopee, Lazada, TikTok Shop, Zalora, JD.ID, Blibli, Zilingo, and Bukalapak. \[web:23]

The integration helps sellers manage stock, update catalogs, process orders, arrange shipping, and respond to marketplace chats from a single dashboard. SIRCLO also supports payment systems, delivery services, automated shipping receipts, website performance tracking, and WhatsApp chat commerce. \[web:18]

**Best for:** Indonesian UMKM that sell on Shopee, Tokopedia, TikTok Shop, or other local marketplaces and want one dashboard for website, marketplace, and chat-based sales.

## Practical Cost Checklist

Before subscribing, a beginner should calculate the real first-year cost, not only the advertised monthly fee.

* **Subscription fee:** Monthly versus annual billing can materially change the total cost.
* **Domain name:** Some plans include a free domain for the first year; renewal is usually charged separately.
* **Payment processing fees:** Check percentage fees, fixed fees, currency-conversion costs, and available local payment methods.
* **Transaction fees:** Some platforms charge extra when using external payment gateways.
* **Apps and add-ons:** Email marketing, reviews, shipping, accounting, and advanced features may require paid apps.
* **Theme or template costs:** Free templates are common, but premium designs can add a one-time or recurring cost.
* **Support and migration:** Ask whether onboarding, data migration, or priority support costs extra.

## Recommendations

For most global beginners, **Wix** is the easiest starting point because of its free plan, drag-and-drop editor, and low-cost entry plans. For businesses whose primary goal is serious product retailing, **Shopify Basic** offers a more commerce-native foundation and a much larger integration ecosystem. For Indonesian UMKM that already sell or plan to sell through local marketplaces, **SIRCLO Store** is often the most operationally relevant choice because it combines website creation with marketplace, payment, delivery, and chat-commerce management. \[web:3]\[web:23]

The best decision is not simply the cheapest plan. Choose the platform that matches where your customers already buy, how you manage inventory, the payment and delivery methods you need, and the level of support you require.
