# Welcome! 👋

This is a multilingual blog built with **Astro** and **Decap CMS**, deployed on **Cloudflare Workers**.

## What you can do

- Write posts in 9 languages: English, 中文, Español, العربية, Bahasa Indonesia, Português, Français, 日本語, and Русский
- Manage all content through the Decap CMS admin panel at `/admin/`
- Automatic right-to-left (RTL) layout for Arabic
- Fast static site generation with Astro
- Free global hosting on Cloudflare

## Getting started

Visit `/admin/` to start managing your content. Sign in with your GitHub account to begin writing posts.
